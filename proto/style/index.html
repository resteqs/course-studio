One look, round 2

Built from your feedback on round 1. Palettes are now generated from one method instead of invented, the objects are redrawn with one light source and real geometry, and the lesson nodes are proper cylinders.

  • Palettes: one recipe (OKLCH scales, tinted neutrals, contrast-checked), three presets, and a Linear-style generator to tune them.
  • Objects: front-facing objects use a single diagonal "paper fold" for light and shade; boxes (chest, ice) are true isometric. Fewer facets, cleaner silhouettes.
  • Nodes: a top face, a visible side wall, a glyph that lies flat on the top, and a ground shadow under the base.
  • Font: Plus Jakarta Sans stays.

What we take from whom

Steal like an artist: take a specific idea from several sources and combine them, so the result is ours and not a copy of any one.

Duolingo

Fixed colours for game objects, chunky tactile controls with a bottom lip, one highlight per object.

Leave: the white canvas, the green brand, cartoon characters.

Brilliant

Restraint: one idea per screen, generous space, precise geometry, colour only where it means something.

Leave: the monochrome line art (too quiet for our reward moments).

Linear

Themes generated from three inputs (base, accent, contrast) in a perceptual colour space, and surfaces that step in small, even increments.

Leave: the productivity-tool density.

Vercel (Geist)

A strict neutral scale with very high text contrast, and typography doing most of the work.

Leave: pure black and white starkness; we keep warmth.

Carrot Weather

A mascot with a voice that reacts to you, and selectable looks as a reward people enjoy collecting.

Leave: the snark; Kubo stays encouraging.

Monument Valley

One light direction everywhere and honest isometric boxes, so objects feel like they sit on the same table.

Leave: the pastel dreaminess.

Palettes from one recipe

Every preset is computed, not picked: a 12-step neutral scale in OKLCH (tighter steps in the middle, wider at the ends), Kubo's indigo as the accent, shared state colours, and four surface levels. The checks below recompute live; every pair must pass 4.5:1. Game-object colours never change.

Object rules

One light, top left

Lit faces point up or left, shaded faces down or right. Ground shadows fall slightly down and to the right, flat under the contact point.

Front objects fold once

Gem, bolt, flame, heart, medal and trophy split along one diagonal into a lit and a shaded half. One highlight, no gradients, no outlines.

Boxes are isometric

The chest and the ice cube use true 30° isometry with three visible faces: top lightest, left middle, right darkest.

Fixed colours

XP yellow, gems pink, streak orange, freezes ice blue, mistakes red, in every palette.

Game objects, redrawn

Compare with round 1: simpler silhouettes, one fold instead of scattered facets, and boxes you can read as boxes.
