One look for the whole app

A proposal to unify everything you see: one palette system, one illustration grammar for game objects, and one tactile UI grammar for controls. Pick a palette and a font at the top; every element on this page follows. Game objects keep fixed colours in every palette, the way Duolingo's hearts are always red and XP is always yellow, so they read as collectibles.

Style rules

Five rules every new icon, object and control follows, so the medal, the chest and the streak flame finally look like they come from the same game.

Flat, faceted fills

Each shape uses three tones: base, a lit facet on the top or left, and a shaded facet on the right or bottom. No gradients, no outlines.

One highlight, one shadow

A single soft white highlight at the top left, and a flat ellipse shadow under objects that sit on the ground.

Fixed object colours

XP is yellow, gems are pink, the streak is orange, freezes are ice blue, mistakes are red. Palettes only change chrome and accent.

Tactile controls

Cards and tiles have a 2px border with a 4–5px bottom lip. Buttons sink 4px when pressed and never lift on hover.

Chunky geometry

Rounded corners on a 4px scale (12, 16, 20). Icons on a 24px grid, objects on a 64px grid, glyphs filled rather than stroked.

Game objects

The full set in one grammar. Today the chest is a flat brown box, the medal is a radial-gradient coin and the flame is a single-colour icon; here they share facets, highlight and shadow.

Reference: how Duolingo does it

Captured from a guest session today. Note the 2px borders with a thicker bottom lip on every tile, the colour-coded feedback sheet, the flat characters with a single highlight, and how the heart keeps its red no matter the screen.

  • Every tile and button: a 2px border with a thicker bottom lip, rounded about 16px, sinking on press.
  • Answer feedback: a full-width sheet tinted green or red, a round icon, the correct solution, and one big button.
  • Characters and objects: flat shapes, a lit and a shaded side, one white highlight, a soft ground shadow, no outlines.
  • Fixed object colours on every screen: red hearts, yellow XP, orange streak, blue gems, purple premium.
  • Chrome stays neutral (white or light grey there), so colour always means a state or a reward.
